About Us

Education & Schools

British values have been highly integrated within the City of Westminster College and Westminster City School curricula. Democracy has been emphasized through learner voice and mock elections, developing critical thinking and responsible citizenship.

Community & Government

Interest-free banking and socially responsible approaches provide social, economic, and environmental progress. Initiatives like increasing apprenticeships and sponsoring community arts projects help build understanding and openness.

Cultural Involvement

Promoting free debate, volunteering, and community projects teaches the importance of tolerance and respect. Active participation in civic life helps reinforce this message to the next generation.
